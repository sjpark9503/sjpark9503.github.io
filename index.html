About

I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Microsoft Research Cambridge. I received my PhD from KAIST, advised by Edward Choi, and my Master's degree from KAIST under the guidance of Sooyoung Lee. During my studies, I had the pleasure of interning at Microsoft Research Asia in Beijing with Xiao Liu and in Vancouver with Yuhang He.

My current research interests lie in multimodal learning and reinforcement learning for remote collaboration environments.
